Our Genesis

From Dorm Room Experiments to State Championship Podiums.

What started as three passionate engineers — an Electrical & Electronics innovator with a DST national grant, a high-altitude UAV flight specialist, and an AI Systems architect — has rapidly matured into a full-spectrum hardware startup.

Bromatrix bridges the deep divide between university lectures and physical industrial execution. We do not just build slides or abstract theories; we manufacture working avionics, mill customized carbon-PLA drone frames, program bare-metal STM32 and ESP32 registers, and stream real-time IoT telemetry over secured TLS MQTT pipelines.

Silicon-Level Rigor

Every board, transceiver, and gyro is bench-tested. Our students and collaborators interact directly with I2C/SPI signals, FreeRTOS tasks, and flight control loops.

Autonomous Flight Autonomy

Custom quadcopter aerodynamics, Betaflight PID configurations, high-voltage LiPo power distribution, and GPS return-to-home fail-safe mechanics.

Mentorship & Impact

Over 400 school and college students mentored through immersive multi-day bootcamps, igniting a genuine engineering mindset across campuses.

CORE CAPABILITIES

What We Build & Engineer

Bromatrix operates across four tightly-integrated hardware disciplines to create robust, deployable systems.

Autonomous UAV Drones

Aeronautical flight stacks featuring STM32F405 flight controllers, Betaflight 4.4, PX4 autopilot, MPU6000 6-axis gyro calibration, BLHeli_S 30A ESCs, and autonomous GPS waypoint guidance.

Connected IoT Telemetry

Real-time sensor telemetry pipelines leveraging dual-core ESP32 microcontrollers, FreeRTOS pre-emptive task scheduling, TLS 1.3 cryptography, and sub-40ms cloud dashboard ingestion.
