STRUCTURED 10-DAY CURRICULUM

Inside the 10-Day Hardware Bootcamp

Explore the day-by-day rigorous engineering progression completed during the Bromatrix Internship 2.0.

Day 01

Foundations & MCU Architecture

Inaugural orientation, toolchain setup, ESP32 dual-core Xtensa pinout mapping, and low-level GPIO register programming.

Day 02

Sensors & Telemetry Ingestion

ADC calibration, analog sampling, temperature/humidity sensors (DHT22), ultrasonic distance ranging, and noise filtering.

Day 03

Firmware Architecture & FreeRTOS

Real-Time Operating System tasks, hardware timers, ISR (Interrupt Service Routines), and asynchronous C/C++ firmware pipelines.

Day 04

IoT Telemetry & MQTT Pipelines

Wi-Fi station configuration, TLS sockets, publish/subscribe MQTT broker clustering, and live web telemetry dashboards.

Day 05

Drone Aerodynamics & PID Tuning

Quadcopter frame dynamics, thrust-to-weight ratios, 6-axis IMU gyro calibration, and Betaflight flight controller tuning.

Day 06

Propulsion, ESCs & RF Link

Brushless DC motors, DShot600 electronic speed controllers, LiPo battery power distribution boards, and 2.4GHz RF pairing.

Day 07

Autonomous Robotics & Kinematics

Dual H-bridge motor drivers, differential drive geometry, IR array sensor bar, and closed-loop PID autonomous track following.

Day 08

3D CAD Modeling in Fusion 360

Parametric sketching, mechanical constraint engineering, shell thickness, snap-fit tolerances, and custom hardware enclosures.

Day 09

Slicing & Additive Manufacturing

G-code generation, layer heights, infill densities, FDM 3D printer calibration, bed leveling, and rapid prototype fabrication.

Day 10

Arena Trials & Certification

Live flight testing arena, Roborace speed trial challenge, capstone technical presentation, and verified credential awards.
